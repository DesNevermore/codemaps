Spawn parallel divergence-HUNTERS — independent sub-agents that each probe the oracle vs your candidate along a distinct lens (arg-parsing, Unicode/width, structured-input, numeric, flag-combos, malformed input) and record every divergence they find into your regression corpus. Use this when your own differential probing dries up and you've run out of ideas: the hunters find the blind spot you missed. After it returns, call `run_regression` and fix each new case.
